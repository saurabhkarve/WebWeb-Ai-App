# Privacy Policy – WebWeb Ai

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

WebWeb Ai ("we", "the app") is a business network for local entrepreneurs, job seekers and customers in India.
This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. **Replace the contact details at the bottom with your own and host this page at a public URL before submitting to Google Play.**

## What we collect
| Data | Why | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| **Mobile number** | Sign-in (we send an OTP by SMS) and to identify your account | Yes |
| **Profile details you enter**: name / company name, username, role, industry, area / location (chosen from a list), services, products, skills, experience, education, photos you upload | Show your profile to other users, match asks and search | Yes (name, username, location); others optional |
| **Contacts you select** from your phone book when you "refer" someone to an ask | Share the names and numbers you picked with the person who posted the ask. We do **not** upload your whole phone book; only the contacts you tick and confirm | Optional |
| **Messages, call history, asks, replies, updates** you create | Provide chat, calling and the community feed | Yes (for those features) |
| **Photos** you pick from your gallery | Product, service and ask pictures | Optional |
| **Microphone and camera** | Voice/video calls. Audio and video go directly between you and the other person (WebRTC); we do not record calls | Optional |
| **Push notification token** | Deliver message and call notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Optional |

## Who can see what
- Your profile, asks, updates and replies are visible to other users of the app.
- A phone number you refer on an ask is visible only to **the person who posted the ask and to you**. Others only see that a contact was shared.
- Chats are visible only to you and the other person. "Delete chat" removes it from your account only.

## Sharing
We do not sell your data. We use service providers to run the app: Google Firebase (push notifications), an SMS provider (one-time passwords), and our hosting / database provider.

## Retention and deletion
Your data stays while your account exists. To delete your account and data, email us at the address below from your registered details, or use the in-app option when available. We delete your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

## Security
Data is sent over HTTPS and stored on managed infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
