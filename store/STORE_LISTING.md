# Store listing – WebWeb Ai

**App name (max 30):** WebWeb Ai: Local Business Network

**Short description (max 80):**
Find local businesses, jobs and people. Ask, refer contacts, chat and call.

**Full description (max 4000):**
WebWeb Ai connects local entrepreneurs, job seekers and customers in your area.

• ASK THE NETWORK – Post what you need (manpower, suppliers, buyers, logistics, funding, advice). People nearby reply with an opinion or refer a trusted contact straight from their phone book.
• REFER CONTACTS – Pick one or many contacts, review them, confirm. Only the person who asked sees the numbers.
• FIND – Search businesses, products & services, jobs, asks and people by name, skill, area or industry.
• TALK – Real-time chat with typing and read receipts, plus voice and video calls and call history.
• YOUR PROFILE – Entrepreneurs add products, services, location (OpenStreetMap), pincode and GSTIN. Job seekers add resume details and skills.
• UPDATES – Share quick news with your network.
• Light and dark themes.

Sign in with just your mobile number (OTP by SMS).
